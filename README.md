### About Me

Third-year Information Science and Engineering student at **Canara Engineering College, Mangalore**.

Most of what I've built started at a hackathon. I like the constraint — you get 24 hours, a real problem, and no room to overthink. It's taught me more about shipping than any tutorial has.

- 🏆 Led a 4-member team to **3rd place** at Project Sankalp "Code4Change", a national 24-hour hackathon
- ⚡ Four hackathons in the last year, from 6-hour sprints to 36-hour builds
- 🎯 Drawn to problems where the hard part isn't the code — voice interfaces for people who can't type, multilingual apps for people who don't read English, offline support for places the network doesn't reach
- 🔨 Comfortable across the stack: Java and OOP fundamentals on one end, React and Node on the other

### Featured Projects

**🌿 [GreenPath](https://greenpath-hs5x.onrender.com)** — *3rd Place, Project Sankalp National Hackathon*
Shows real-time air quality and suggests cleaner travel routes, with voice guidance in English, Kannada, and Hindi and offline access for weak-network areas. Led a 4-member team from idea to working prototype in 24 hours.
`Node.js` `JavaScript` `Google Maps API`

**🗣️ [Awaaz](https://team-26-1.onrender.com)** — *SRISHTI 2026, National 36-Hour Hackathon*
Lets people report grievances anonymously by speaking in their own language, without needing an account. Complaints are tracked by reference ID and locked with a Solidity smart contract so records can't be altered later.
`JavaScript` `Node.js` `Solidity`

**🆘 [KaliSOS](https://kali-sos.vercel.app)** — *AETHERION-26, 24-Hour Hackathon*
Sends an emergency alert when the user says "help me" out loud, so nobody has to reach for their phone. Streams live GPS to a responder dashboard that shows the caller on a map in real time.
`React` `Node.js` `MySQL`

**☕ [Mess Management System](https://github.com/krithu2006/mess-management-system)**
Manages college mess registration with auto-generated tokens, fee verification, and meal-wise attendance tracking. Built in Java using inheritance, encapsulation, and polymorphism.
`Java`
