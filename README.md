# Kritharth Jain

I build web apps for people the usual interfaces leave out — voice input for those who can't type, three languages instead of one, offline-first for places the network doesn't reach. Mostly with **React**, **Node.js**, **MySQL**, and **Java**.

Third-year Information Science & Engineering student at Canara Engineering College, Mangalore.

---

### Selected work

**[GreenPath](https://greenpath-hs5x.onrender.com)** — real-time air quality with cleaner-route suggestions, voice-guided in English, Kannada and Hindi, works offline.
*3rd place nationally at Project Sankalp Code4Change. I led the 4-person team.*

**[Awaaz](https://team-26-1.onrender.com)** — grievance reporting you can do by speaking, anonymously, in your own language, with no account. Records locked with a Solidity contract so they can't be edited after the fact.

**[KaliSOS](https://kali-sos.vercel.app)** — say "help me" out loud and it fires an alert with live GPS to a responder dashboard. No unlocking, no tapping.

**[mess-management-system](https://github.com/krithu2006/mess-management-system)** — replaces the paper token-and-attendance register every college mess still runs on. Java, built on OOP fundamentals.
